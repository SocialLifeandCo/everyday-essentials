Owner controls

Make the website your own.

Build-Only includes owner settings to personalize all of the following. Done-for-You and higher packages also include owner-editable settings.

  • Business name
  • Logo
  • Colors and fonts
  • Phone and email
  • Address/service area and hours
  • Services and pricing
  • Photos
  • About section and team/bios
  • Testimonials and FAQs
  • Social links
  • Buttons/calls to action
  • Announcements
  • Basic SEO fields

Build-Only includes search-friendly structure and editable fields. Done-for-You includes search-ready headings, metadata framework and service/business information using your supplied content. This website foundation is separate from the $249 AI Search Foundation optimization service, which includes research, optimization of up to 5 existing pages, testing and a before-and-after report. Full research, expanded optimization and monthly visibility plans are separate paid services.
