Clear expectations

Visibility work with a defined scope.

These services improve how clearly your business can be understood online. Google rankings, ChatGPT recommendations, AI placement, traffic increases, leads and revenue are never guaranteed. AI search test results are snapshots and may vary by tool, wording, location and date.

Appropriate structured data is added only where supported and does not guarantee search features or AI recommendations. Location content reflects places you genuinely serve. We handle the agreed research, content and website updates; your role is to provide business facts and access, review recommendations and approve changes.

Packages focus on website research and optimization. They exclude manual backlink building, purchased links, bulk directory submissions, daily social posting, weekly Google Business posts, ongoing review-response or reputation management, and high-volume blogging.

Website page limits are total limits, not cumulative across package levels. Scope, platform access and qualifying audit-credit eligibility are confirmed before work begins. Monthly visibility plans are separate from website Managed Support and store-support plans.
