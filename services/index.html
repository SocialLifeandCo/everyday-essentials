Service policies

Clear expectations from the start.

Payment

A 50% deposit is required to begin. The remaining 50% is due before final launch and ownership transfer.

Turnaround & revisions

Turnaround begins after payment, content, photos, business information, and account details arrive. Revision rounds depend on the package. Out-of-scope additions may need a new quote.

14-day correction period

For 14 calendar days after launch, we correct technical issues with the website as delivered: broken links, display/layout issues, form errors, and delivered features that do not work. It excludes new content, pages, redesigns, features, client-made or third-party changes, and issues caused after delivery.
