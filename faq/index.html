FAQ & policies

Good to know before we begin.

Answers about shopping, custom requests, and business services.

Your questions

Helpful details in one place.

Where can I shop products?

Choose a collection on the Shop page. Working category links open Payhip for browsing and checkout; pending categories are clearly marked.

How do I request a custom order?

Send the details through the Custom Order Request form and we’ll follow up about the quote and next steps.

How do business service payments work?

A 50% deposit is due to begin and the remaining 50% is due before final launch and ownership transfer.

When does the timeline begin?

After required payment, content, photos, business information, and account details have been received.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds depend on the package. Out-of-scope additions may need a new quote.

What does the 14-day correction period cover?

For 14 calendar days after launch, technical issues with the originally delivered website are corrected, including broken links, layout issues, form errors, and delivered features that do not work. New content, pages, redesigns, features, client-made or third-party changes, and later-caused issues are excluded.

Are Shopify fees included?

No. Shopify subscription fees, domain costs, paid apps, payment-processing fees, and other third-party fees are paid directly by the client.
